## MasterPrompt:
Build the front end of FlightFood, a web product for an airline catering company. Their job on this
product is to allow passengers to select the food that they want on their flight. Screens:
[SCREEN 1: Shows the food options available for their booked flight]
[SCREEN 2, Indicate any Special Meal Requests such as Vegetarian, Halal, Non Beef]
[SCREEN 3, Miscellaneous Meal Requests that are not guaranteed to be served]
[SCREEN 4: Confirmation page summarizing selected preferences and order status]
